The day they learned to choose each other

Jennifer always thought love was something that simply happened. Meeting Andrés was easy, natural—almost as if it had already been written. But what she never imagined was that loving also meant deciding.

After two years together, the first serious arguments came. Differences in money, time, expectations. Everything seemed to shake.

One night, in the middle of an uncomfortable silence, Andrés said: "Loving you isn't always easy… but it is always my choice."

That day they understood something: love is not just emotion, it is commitment. It's not just feeling—it's choosing.

Today, married, they still have differences. But they also have something stronger: the daily decision to keep saying "yes."
