More than a magazine

Said Yes Magazine is born from a simple but honest observation. Today, much of wedding content looks impeccable, but it often lacks a true emotional connection. Everything is carefully curated, but it does not always convey what it really means.

We are a modern wedding magazine that goes beyond the visual. A platform where weddings are not only seen, but felt. Where stories carry the same weight as aesthetics.

More than following a traditional editorial model, Said Yes Magazine is conceived as a living platform, with continuous content that combines editorial, short-form video, and podcast, all under one vision.

At its core, the brand is centered on real stories. Not only perfect weddings, but honest experiences, important decisions, and those moments that almost no one talks about.

Said Yes Magazine is also a positive and intentional space. Weddings represent commitment, values, and, for many people, a deep connection with faith.

Meet our team

At the heart of every issue is a collective of storytellers, perfectionists, and creative rebels. Our team is a diverse blend of editorial precision and visual flair, dedicated to capturing the pulse of culture and bringing it straight to your hands. We do not just report on trends, we live them, curate them, and challenge them.
